    Learn · practise · review

    A session with purpose

    Suggested session outline. The coach can adjust the activities to the group and available time.

    01

    Check in & warm up

    Take attendance, share the session goal, then begin movement and ball-based warm-ups.

    02

    Build the basics

    Practise first touch, short passing, dribbling and controlled finishing.

    03

    Learn your role

    Work in small groups on defending, midfield support, attacking movement or goalkeeping.

    04

    Play & apply

    Use small-sided games to practise communication, positioning and decisions under pressure.

    05

    Cool down & reflect

    Finish together, discuss what improved and choose one skill to work on next time.

    Come prepared

    Your first training day

    • Bring drinking water, training clothes, suitable football footwear and shin guards.
    • Be ready to provide your name, age, contact details, preferred roles and emergency contact for registration.
    • Tell the coach privately about any injury or condition relevant to participation.
    • If under 18, arrange parent or guardian consent with the coordinator.
    • Come ready to listen, practise and encourage others.

    Registration and health details should be shared privately, not posted on this website or in the group.

    Our training culture

    Progress comes from practice

    • Be punctual and report absences.
    • Respect teammates, coaches and different ability levels.
    • Share opportunities and be willing to try another role.
    • Use feedback to improve; mistakes are part of learning.
    • Care for equipment and keep the training area tidy.
