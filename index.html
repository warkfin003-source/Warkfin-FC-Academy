About us

Purpose, discipline and development.

We want the habits learned in training to make a difference on and off the field.

Who we are

Warkfin FC Academy is a developing football training community bringing together students, non-students aged 18 and above, and adults who want to improve their game.

Our current training group is G.O.A.T. Squad A. Participation arrangements for under-18s must be confirmed with the coordinator and a parent or guardian.

What we do

We focus on football fundamentals, positional understanding, physical preparation and teamwork through practical sessions and small-sided games.

Our aim is steady development, with room to learn another position and grow in confidence.

Our Team

Founder / Coordinator: Emmanuel Mmesoma Adinna.

Our 17 listed players bring a mix of attacking, midfield, defensive and goalkeeping interests. Coaching and support appointments will be announced when confirmed.

Meet G.O.A.T. Squad A

Our Plans

Our proposed next steps are to establish consistent training, assess players fairly, strengthen defensive coverage and build a reliable coaching and support structure.

Practice fixtures and additional training groups may follow as the academy develops. No competition entries or professional placements are currently promised.

Our Approach

Start from each player's current ability. Demonstrate clearly, allow repeated practice, offer useful feedback and recognise progress.

We value effort, respect and reliability alongside football ability. Students and adults should train in groups appropriate to age, ability and session needs.

Our Training Methodology

  1. Show: explain and demonstrate the skill.
  2. Practise: repeat in manageable drills.
  3. Apply: use it in a game situation.
  4. Review: give feedback and choose the next goal.

What progress looks like

A better first touch. A well-timed supporting run. Clearer communication. Consistent attendance. These are meaningful improvements.

Players will be encouraged to reflect on one strength and one skill to practise next. Positions remain open to assessment during training.

    A session with purpose

    Suggested session outline. The coach can adjust the activities to the group and available time.

    01

    Check in & warm up

    Take attendance, share the session goal, then begin movement and ball-based warm-ups.

    02

    Build the basics

    Practise first touch, short passing, dribbling and controlled finishing.

    03

    Learn your role

    Work in small groups on defending, midfield support, attacking movement or goalkeeping.

    04

    Play & apply

    Use small-sided games to practise communication, positioning and decisions under pressure.

    05

    Cool down & reflect

    Finish together, discuss what improved and choose one skill to work on next time.

    Football guide

    Understand the game. Train with intention.

    Practical reminders to support coaching—not replace the coach's instructions.

    Before you take the field

    • Bring your complete kit, suitable footwear, shin guards and drinking water.
    • Check the announced venue and time; arrive ready for the session.
    • Tell the coach about any problem that affects your participation.
    • Follow the group warm-up and listen to the session goal.

    Make practice count

    • Choose one skill to improve each session.
    • Start with controlled technique before adding speed or pressure.
    • Ask for feedback and apply one clear correction at a time.
    • Finish by identifying what improved and what needs another attempt.

    What does 4–3–3 mean?

    Four defenders, three midfielders and three forwards, with a goalkeeper behind them. The goalkeeper is not included in the three formation numbers.

    Our board is a learning illustration. Positions, spacing and responsibilities can change during practice; being listed in a role does not guarantee a starting place.

    Simple team habits

    In possession: offer passing options, use available space and move after passing.

    Out of possession: communicate, stay connected to teammates and work together to protect the goal.

    When possession changes: react, look around and recognise whether your team needs support in attack or defence.

    Respect and fair play

    Respect teammates, opponents, coaches and match officials. Avoid insults, fighting and deliberate dangerous play. Raise disagreements calmly with the coach or coordinator.

    Encourage less experienced players, share equipment responsibly and leave the field tidy.

    Player information

    Before your first session.

    Can I train in a different position?

    Yes—tell the coach which role you want to learn. Preferred positions are starting points, and training gives you an opportunity to practise alternatives.

    Does printing the form complete registration?

    No. The form is a preparation sheet only. Hand it privately to the coordinator and confirm participation arrangements directly. The website does not send or store completed forms.

    What are the fees and age requirements?

    Fees, if any, have not been confirmed on this website. The academy welcomes students and adults; arrangements for anyone under 18 must be confirmed with the coordinator and a parent or guardian before participation.

    How do I report an absence or ask about a change?

    Contact the coordinator through the academy WhatsApp group. Check the latest dated announcement before travelling and report an absence as early as possible.

    Does the academy guarantee professional contracts?

    No. The academy focuses on training and player development. No professional contract, scholarship, trial invitation or scouting opportunity is guaranteed.

    Small habits. Better football.

    Take these into your next session.

    Look before you receive

    Check where teammates, opponents and space are before the ball reaches you.

    Pass, then move

    After a pass, find a useful angle so your teammate has another option.

    Practise both feet

    Use simple, controlled touches and passes to build confidence on either side.

    Communicate positively

    Give clear, useful calls. Encourage the next attempt instead of dwelling on a mistake.
